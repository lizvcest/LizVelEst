About
me

Hi, I’m Liz.

I’m a digital marketer who likes to understand how things work, from the strategy behind a campaign down to the tracking that proves it worked.

Over the past 8 years I’ve managed campaigns for many clients across restaurants, SaaS, real estate and legal services. I handle the whole cycle: planning the strategy, building campaigns in Google Ads and Meta, setting up conversion tracking, and automating lead flows into the CRM so no lead gets lost.

I’m detail-oriented by nature. I’m the person who catches the broken tracking tag before it costs a month of data.
